Android App

ILAALO

Automatically sweeps incoming ZAAD / eDahab deposits to your chosen account, so your balance is never left exposed.

Download APK

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. Open ILAALO, create your account, and follow the setup steps.
  4. Enable the ILAALO Accessibility service and allow SMS and Phone permissions.

Privacy

Your PINs are stored only on your phone in encrypted storage and are never sent anywhere. Only basic transaction information (amount, status, masked number) is synced to your account.
